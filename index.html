ABOUT VU ROVERS

Who We Are

VU Rovers is the official rover scout group of Varendra University. We aim to develop leadership, character, skills and teamwork through adventure, service and lifelong friendship.

Adventure

Explore new places and take part in exciting challenges and activities.

Teamwork

Work together, support each other and achieve goals as one team.

Service

Serve the community and contribute positively to humanity.

Excellence

Strive for excellence in every activity, responsibility and challenge.
